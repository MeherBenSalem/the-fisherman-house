# Patch notes

## 2.0.0 — The Living Coast

The Fisherman House becomes Fisherman's Coast: six new structures (Harbour Pier, Lighthouse, Stilt Shack, Ice Fishing
Camp, River Boathouse, Sunken Trawler) plus the repaired Fisherman's Cottage, all placed by shore-aware placement;
named residents with exclusive trades; Crab Pots with biome catches, bait, rain bonus and hopper support; Fish Bait,
Chum and Glow Bait; Messages in a Bottle that lead to sunken trawlers; Fishing Nets; loot tables, advancements, a
config and an inspect command. Available for Minecraft 1.20.1 (Fabric, Forge), 1.21.1, 26.1.2, 26.2 and 26.3 (Fabric,
NeoForge); the 1.21.1 Forge build is dropped. Worlds from 1.x keep loading.

Full notes: [patchnotes/2.0.0.md](patchnotes/2.0.0.md).
